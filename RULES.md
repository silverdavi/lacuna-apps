# Rules

## Contest

This is a skill contest for an app merged into this repository between 29 October 2026 and 29 November 2026, ending at 23:59 America/New_York. A random drawing does not pick the winner.

One prize. One person receives it.

## Prize

The prize is a new MacBook Neo, 13-inch, with the Apple A18 Pro chip, 8GB of unified memory, and a 256GB SSD, in a color Apple sells at that configuration's price. The cash alternative is $699.

$699 is Apple's US store price for the 256GB model on 27 October 2026. The store page listed the offer at 699.00 USD: [https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-mac/macbook-neo/silver-256gb](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-mac/macbook-neo/silver-256gb). Chip and memory are on Apple's tech specs: [https://support.apple.com/en-us/126322](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126322). If Apple's US price for that configuration changes before these rules are published, replace $699 with the new Apple price and record the date in the published version.

Lacuna pays shipping of the laptop to an address in the United States. A winner outside the United States receives $699 and does not receive the laptop.

The winner chooses the laptop or the cash. Lacuna buys the laptop from Apple, or from an Apple-authorized reseller, after the winner accepts.

## Eligibility

An entrant is 18 or older on 29 October 2026. The contest is void where prohibited. David Silver, and anyone else Lacuna pays to build the product, cannot win the laptop or the cash.

Five apps ship with this repository: `tex-snippet`, `receipt-packet`, `pomodoro`, `markdown-table`, and `tip-split`. They are first-party. They are not entries.

## Tax

If the winner is a US person and the prize value is over $600, Lacuna issues Form 1099. $699 is over $600, so a US winner receives a 1099 for the laptop or for the cash. The winner owes any tax on the prize. Lacuna may ask for a Form W-9 before it sends the prize.

## Judging

The published criteria are:

- Usefulness to other people.
- Module reuse: the app imports toolbox modules.
- Identity rules: tokens for color and type, the naming register, and no separate account system inside the app.
- Polish: light and dark screenshots, keyboard use, and a manifest that validates.

The community vote is 30% of the score. David Silver scores the other 70% on those four criteria and publishes the points with the winner. The vote is a count of public votes on the merged app during the contest window, scaled to 30 points. The place the vote is cast will be named in this file before 29 October 2026, once these rules are no longer a draft.

If two apps tie, David Silver names the winner and writes the reason next to the scores.

## Publicity

Accepting the prize includes consent for Lacuna to publish the winner's name, public handle, and app name in the announcement. A person who does not want that publication declines the prize.

## Intellectual property

The author keeps the copyright. The submission is under the MIT license in this repository. Lacuna builds and serves the app under that license.

## Reporter prizes

These are separate from the laptop. During the same window, the top 20 people with an accepted bug report or an accepted feature request each receive a $20 Amazon e-gift, a $20 Visa e-gift, or reimbursement of one month of Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor Pro. The person picks one of those. Accepted means a maintainer marks the issue accepted. If fewer than 20 reports are accepted, Lacuna pays only the people whose reports were accepted. A person is paid once.

## Budget

$699 for the laptop or the cash alternative, $400 for up to twenty reporter prizes at $20, and shipping if the laptop is sent. That is $1,099 plus shipping.

## Acceptance

Lacuna writes to the email on the winning pull request. The winner has 14 days to accept, choose the laptop or the cash, and, if they take the laptop in the US, send a shipping address and a W-9. If they do not answer, the prize goes to the next-highest score.
