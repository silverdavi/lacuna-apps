# Build challenge

Entries open 29 October 2026 and close 29 November 2026. You enter by opening a pull request that adds `apps/<slug>/` with a valid `lacuna.app.json`, a build, and light and dark screenshots. The pull request template lists the fields. Sign off under the Developer Certificate of Origin in [DCO.txt](DCO.txt). There is no CLA.

The prize is one MacBook Neo (13-inch, Apple A18 Pro, 8GB unified memory, 256GB SSD), or $699 cash. $699 is Apple's US price for that configuration on 27 October 2026 ([Apple Store, 256GB](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-mac/macbook-neo/silver-256gb); [tech specs](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126322)). Lacuna pays shipping when a US winner takes the laptop. A winner outside the US receives the $699 cash equivalent. Eligibility, tax, judging, and the reporter prizes are in [RULES.md](RULES.md).

Those rules are a draft. They need a one-hour lawyer review before they are announced as official.

Copy [template/](template/), or drop [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) into Cursor or Claude Code. Import UI from the toolbox. Use tokens for color and type. Do not call the network. Do not use `eval` or `new Function`.

The five apps already in `apps/` are first-party. Each `OWNERS.md` says so. They do not count as community entries.
